# Work rules entry point

[The BibleQuest rulebook](RULEBOOK.md) is the single operational authority. Read section 1 and only the sections relevant to the task. It consolidates V1–V6 lessons and replaces duplicate/new-work prescriptions in earlier agent protocols; historical evidence and existing release gates remain intact.

- Small change: section 2 and the relevant product section.
- Multi-agent work: section 7.
- Production release: sections 8–9 and [release procedure](RELEASE.md).
- Documentation/cleanup: section 10 and [documentation policy](DOCUMENTATION.md).

User scope and authorization take precedence. No broad audit or full rulebook-sized checklist is required for routine work.
