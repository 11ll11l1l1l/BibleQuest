Recover your account

BibleQuest does not use email confirmation. Your registered email is your sign-in ID. To reset a forgotten password, enter the recovery code you were shown when you created your account or generated later in Account → Security.

Still signed in on another phone or computer?Use that device instead: open your BibleQuest account, then Security & recovery. You can change your password or generate a new recovery code without using this page.
Back to BibleQuest
For security, BibleQuest cannot safely reset an account from an email address alone because the email is not verified. If you lose both your password and recovery code and have no signed-in device, there is no automatic proof that the account belongs to you.
