## Product decision

V7 is the **full BibleQuest overhaul using the certified V6 engine**.

V7 may comprehensively redesign page composition, navigation treatment, information hierarchy, responsive behavior, component presentation, artwork, visual language, interaction patterns, motion, sound and cross-page cohesion.

V7 is not another engine/architecture replacement. It consumes the V6 build/app-kernel/data/tenant/Reader/Games/media/notification/component/motion-sound systems. Engine gaps are fixed at the engine layer rather than bypassed with per-page hacks.
