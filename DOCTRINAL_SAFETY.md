# BibleQuest doctrinal content policy

BibleQuest teaches Scripture first and treats secondary study resources as aids, not doctrinal authorities.

## Authority order

1. Scripture text and passage context.
2. Official CAMACOP Statement of Faith for denominational alignment.
3. Alliance World Fellowship material where it is compatible with CAMACOP and CAMACOP is silent.
4. Pastor-reviewed local teaching for local-church-specific interpretation.
5. Secondary resources such as unfoldingWord, STEPBible, OpenBible.info, and Open Bible Stories as study aids only.

## Question classifications

- `TEXTUAL_FACT`: direct factual or textual recall. Safe for scored play.
- `PASSAGE_CONTEXT`: sensitive or easily misunderstood material. May appear only with explicit passage framing and a context notice.
- `INTERPRETIVE_OR_DOCTRINAL`: a question that can function as a universal doctrine claim. Quarantined from normal play until rewritten or pastor reviewed.
- `APPLICATION`: reflection/wisdom material. Must not be presented as a one-answer doctrine quiz.
- `PASTOR_APPROVED`: reviewed material that may teach a denominational position when its source is recorded.

## Sensitive topics

Questions involving salvation and works, baptism, Communion, Holy Spirit baptism/filling, tongues and gifts, sanctification, healing, election/predestination, eternal security, end times, church office, marriage/sexuality, creation chronology, spiritual warfare, and giving/prosperity receive additional screening.

A sensitive verse is not removed merely because it is difficult. The safety layer distinguishes direct textual recall from a standalone theological conclusion. Questions that could teach doctrine out of context are removed from normal scoring until they are rewritten with sufficient context or explicitly reviewed.

## Imported resources

unfoldingWord Translation Questions remain a useful source of passage-comprehension material, but they are not a CAMACOP catechism. Imported questions must pass BibleQuest's safety classification before they appear in normal recall decks.
