# BibleQuest external data attribution

- Berean Standard Bible / BSB data: public-domain (CC0) components as marked by BSB Publishing.
- unfoldingWord Translation Questions, Translation Notes, Translation Words, Open Bible Stories and OBS Translation Questions: CC BY-SA 4.0; source: unfoldingWord / Door43.
- STEPBible TIPNR proper-names data: CC BY 4.0; credit STEP Bible / Tyndale House Cambridge.
- OpenBible geocoding and cross-reference data: CC BY; credit OpenBible.info.
- World English Bible Updated: public domain, distributed by eBible.org.
- Tagalog ULB: Door43 World Missions Community; retain the upstream copyright/license notices distributed with the pack.

`manifest.json` records pinned resource versions/commits used for each build.
