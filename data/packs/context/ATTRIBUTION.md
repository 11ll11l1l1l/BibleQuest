# Original-language context packs

BibleQuest combines the **Strong’s identifiers carried by the BSB data** with brief lexical fields from **STEPBible TBESH (Hebrew) and TBESG (Greek)**. STEPBible data is credited to STEP Bible / Tyndale House Cambridge and licensed **CC BY 4.0**.

BibleQuest packages lemma, transliteration, morphology, and brief gloss only. These tools show lexical context; they are not a word-for-word interlinear and do not determine the meaning of a verse by themselves.
