# Wisdom Situations — adversarial Christian judgment update

The current and authoritative **72-case / 288-option adversarial review** is in [wisdom-72-adversarial-scrutiny.md](./wisdom-72-adversarial-scrutiny.md). This document summarizes why the first two versions did not meet the user's requested standard.

## Confirmed failures of the earlier content

1. In the original bank, all 72 preferred responses were longest, giving away the intended answer.
2. Replacing straightforward choices with 1 benefit + 2 costs did not make them substantively hard. A fresh word-level red-team check found **84/288** second costs based on blame, regret, generic criticism and similar weak stakes.
3. Several alternatives asked the reader to choose unsafe, nonconsensual, misleading or professionally impermissible conduct. Those were invalid distractors, not legitimate Christian dilemmas.
4. A mere 'preferred response' label implicitly suggested unique spiritual correctness even where duties conflict and the right judgment depends on conditions.
5. Some original narratives contradicted the newer overlay (notably housing guarantee #65).

## Current draft design

- All **72** English cases carry constrained circumstances and **4** option-specific factual grounds each (**288** total).
- Each choice still names an action, a meaningful benefit, an external sacrifice and a direct personal cost. The 84 weak personal costs were rewritten, with hard-coded format and word-length parity checks.
- The client shows each factual ground with the option instead of implying one doctrinally correct letter. After selection, it presents competing explanations and a reflection about involuntary cost, new evidence and accountability. This does not establish equal moral permissibility; negligence, deception and abuse are not made right through clever trade-offs.
- Known scene contradictions and morally invalid distractors have targeted corrections. Further editorial and Scripture-reference verification remains necessary.
- No new XP is granted purely for this revision. Existing case IDs are retained.
- Until substantive reviews of new Tagalog and Cebuano text exist, the app shows the coherent English case rather than mixing an old translated answer with a new English editorial index.

## Release blockers

- **28 flagged high-risk cases** need independent domain review; see the per-question review ledger.
- Blind readers must test **all four** options in every case, defending rejected alternatives and identifying any answer that feels obvious without deliberation. Hardness cannot be verified by static software checks.
- Confirm that each Scripture reference is used in context rather than as a predetermined answer key.
- Verify mobile, accessibility, continuity of progress, full automated tests and browser flows.
- Restore equivalent newly reviewed TL/CEB localization, clearly indicating the English fallback until complete.
