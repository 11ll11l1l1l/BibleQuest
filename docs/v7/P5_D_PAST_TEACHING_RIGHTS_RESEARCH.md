## Rights evidence located

### Historical sermon text

UECF's edition page for the same sermon identifies it as Sermon 2002 and states that the underlying pulpit texts used for that collection are public-domain sermon texts prepared from the Spurgeon Gems Metropolitan Tabernacle / New Park Street collection:

- sermon: `https://www.uecf.net/books/the-power-of-prayer-in-a-believers-life/chapter2.html`
- collection/source note: `https://www.uecf.net/books/the-power-of-prayer-in-a-believers-life/about.html`

This supports the proposition that the historical sermon text is treated as public-domain material, but it should not automatically be treated as a license to copy UECF's modern site presentation or any protectable editorial additions.

### CCEL is not a clean unrestricted electronic-source basis

CCEL hosts *Spurgeon's Sermons Volume 34: 1888* and states generally that most underlying books in its library are public domain in the United States:

- work page: `https://ccel.org/ccel/spurgeon/sermons34.html`
- copyright policy: `https://www.ccel.org/about/copyright.html`

However, CCEL's generated PDF for this work states that the PDF file itself is copyrighted by CCEL, allows free copying for non-commercial purposes if unmodified, reserves other rights, and requires written permission for commercial use:

- `https://www.ccel.org/ccel/s/spurgeon/sermons34/cache/sermons34.pdf`

Therefore the CCEL electronic edition should **not** be used as the unrestricted rights basis for BibleQuest display unless the intended use fits those terms or separate permission is obtained.

### Public-domain scan precedent

Wikimedia Commons hosts scanned volumes of *The Metropolitan Tabernacle Pulpit* and marks those historical works public domain in the country of origin where the term is life + 70 years or fewer, and in the United States because they were published before 1931. Example:

- `https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Spurgeon%27s_Sermons_-_Vol._XI-_The_Metropolitan_Tabernacle_Pulpit_(IA_SpurgeonMetropolitanPt11).pdf`

This is useful precedent for the historical series, but the research performed for this packet did not locate an equally explicit rights-marked Commons/Internet Archive record for **Volume 34** itself. Do not substitute an adjacent volume as rights evidence for the exact source edition.

## Recommended evidence boundary for approval

Before changing `rights.status` from `unknown` to `verified`, an authorized reviewer should pin at least one exact-source basis that clearly supports the intended BibleQuest use. Preferred evidence, in order:

1. an exact Volume 34 / Sermon 2002 facsimile or transcription carrying an explicit Public Domain Mark or equivalent unrestricted-rights statement; or
2. a documented legal/editorial determination that the BibleQuest record only summarizes the 1888 public-domain work, does not reproduce protectable modern-edition expression, and that the selected source URI is used for provenance/linking rather than copied site content.

If approved, the repository record should preserve the exact `evidenceUri`, rights holder/basis, allowed uses, verification date, reviewer evidence, and publication decision. The current source URI may remain useful for provenance even if a separate rights-evidence URI is selected.
