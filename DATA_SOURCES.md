# BibleQuest content-source strategy

BibleQuest should prefer public-domain or openly licensed structured resources and retain provenance on every imported record.

## Primary planned sources

- Berean Standard Bible (BSB) — public domain Bible text.
- World English Bible (WEB) — public domain Bible text and fallback translation.
- unfoldingWord Translation Questions — CC BY-SA 4.0 structured chapter/verse questions and answers.
- unfoldingWord Open Bible Stories — open-licensed narrative material for Story Adventure.
- unfoldingWord Translation/Study Notes — contextual material with provenance and content classification.
- STEPBible Data — CC BY 4.0 datasets for people, places, names and original-language metadata.
- OpenBible.info cross references — open cross-reference data for connection games.

## Content rules

1. Preserve source name, source version, source URL, license and attribution for imports.
2. Classify content as fact, context, interpretation, wisdom or open question.
3. AI may transform verified source material into game formats but must not silently invent the underlying biblical fact.
4. ShareAlike material stays traceable and separable from BibleQuest-original code/content.
5. Bible translations with restrictive redistribution terms are not bundled without confirmed permission.

The current public alpha uses a small starter set while the bulk import pipeline is being built.
